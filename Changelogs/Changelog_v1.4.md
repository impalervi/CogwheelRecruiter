# Cogwheel Recruiter - Changelog v1.4

## WoW: Forever Support
- Cogwheel Recruiter now works in World of Warcraft: Forever.
- Scanner and Quick Scanner work with Forever's /who rules (one search per click).
- Zones updated for Forever: added Zephras Isle, Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Mount Hyjal and the Darkspear Islands battleground; removed Outland and other Burning Crusade zones.
- Level filters go up to 60.
- Full character names (first and last name) are used for whispers, invites, whisper replies, and guild join/decline tracking.
- The minimap button sits on the edge of Forever's larger minimap and can be dragged around it like other addon buttons. New installs start it at the bottom left.
- Window buttons match Forever's style and no longer show through other windows.

## All Versions
- Updated for the latest game versions.
- Fixed: after moving the minimap button, the next click on it was ignored.
